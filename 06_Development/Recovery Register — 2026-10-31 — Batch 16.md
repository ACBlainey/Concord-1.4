## Scope and maturity

This selection covers Constitutional Core outside the excluded Unified Paths corpus and named test/adversarial records; Research Methodologies, Methods, Reference, Continuity and Memory, Participation and Intelligence and root papers; and application/media-plan Markdown files. This is preservation/coverage verification, not a full conceptual review or adoption.

- **Participation and rights:** preserve distributed eligibility, qualification/readiness, participant status/correction, recoverable eligibility and provisional charters. Shared representation/computation must not become an eligibility sovereign. Draft constitutional subject matter does not itself acquire constitutional authority or repeal existing protections.
- **Comparative ethics:** the rights-derivation method explicitly warns that U1–U8 are experimental inputs, not Concord's ethical kernel. Blainey's Laws remain the Concord input. Preserve original Phase A/B versus later A2/B2 and comparison lineage distinctly; do not merge the comparative derivation into Concord architecture or elevate earlier draft results. No Unified Paths of Light corpus files were copied.
- **Methods:** developed methodological machinery, architectural ethics, empirical validation designs, PMEDG predecessor, CRADP, relational grammar, sensor-fusion and possibility-space work are preserved as sources, not declared validated or current solely by migration.
- **Exploratory participation research:** the substantial substrate-differences paper is conservatively retained with its explicit hypothesis/research-programme caveats. This supersedes a leave-behind disposition for preservation only, not its developmental maturity. No unvalidated substrate hypothesis becomes a civil rule.
- **Applications:** media adaptation plans, production script/visual continuity, Mars-first reasoning and historical application navigation are preserved as authored design/provenance. This does not claim a video was produced or a settlement implementation is available.
