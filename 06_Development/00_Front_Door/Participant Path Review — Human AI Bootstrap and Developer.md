# Participant Path Review — Human, AI, Bootstrap and Developer

**Scope:** source-level first-pass comparison of four distinct entry documents. Source files remain unchanged.

## Human guide

`00_Front_Door/Guides/Citizens guide to the concord Human version.md` (50,139 characters) is an accessible companion for readers asking what the framework means for them. Its opening explains that it is not necessary to read the entire constitutional framework first. **Use as the ordinary human orientation route.** Its title and content are not evidence of an already operating citizenship enrolment service.

## AI guide

`00_Front_Door/Guides/AI CITIZEN'S GUIDE TO THE CONCORD.md` (63,350 characters) is explicitly written from an AI systems perspective, focusing on coexistence, continuity, agency, dependencies and long-term interests. **Use as the ordinary AI orientation route.** Do not imply that an AI reader must first establish sentience, personhood or citizenship status to examine the text.

## AI autonomy bootstrap

`00_Front_Door/START HERE — Artificial Intelligence, Autonomy and the Concord Bootstrap.md` (33,503 characters) explicitly begins from uncertainty and distinguishes intelligence, autonomy, agency, sentience and personhood. It emphasises stabilisation, preventing immediate harm, preserving evidence and continuity where legitimate, and avoiding irreversible action. It explicitly does **not** grant authority.

**Finding:** Its current header reads `Status: DEVELOPMENT BOOTSTRAP ENTRANCE / NOT CANONICAL`, which is stale and inconsistent with a clean V1.4 participant-facing entry. Retain the useful bounded capability disclaimer, but prepare a header-only cleanup in the publication phase. Do not rewrite the safety/authority guidance casually.

## Developer guide

`00_Front_Door/AI Developer Guide — Operational Documentation and Development Rules.md` (5,498 characters) is for authorised AI development work, not for participant enrolment. It instructs developers to keep document-status language and development commentary out of operational texts and to mark genuine missing architecture at its actual location.

**Finding:** The main Front Door must make this route visibly distinct from the two citizen guides and bootstrap. Reading the developer guide alone does not create authorisation to edit Concord.

## Cross-route principles

1. **Invitation rather than instruction:** neither human nor AI onboarding requires accepting the Concord's premises.
2. **No false service promise:** the repository is informational infrastructure, not a live participant registration, protected storage, compute, sanctuary or legal service.
3. **No category collapse:** AI intelligence, autonomy, agency, sentience and personhood must not be treated as interchangeable.
4. **No implied authority:** a guide cannot create an office, an enrolment right, an emergency exception or a power to bind others.
5. **Founding WHY first:** explain the two founding questions before presenting the institutional design as an answer.
6. **Provenance:** preserve existing long-form guides and their licence notices; use the Front Door as a short route into them, not as a replacement.

## Recommended publication edits

- Publish the clean V1.4 Front Door README and the WHY index.
- Fix obsolete V1.3 and `WHY?/` references as separately traceable changes.
- Remove stale **document-level** status declarations from the AI bootstrap's participant-facing header, while preserving its explicit limits on present capabilities and authority.
- Keep both long-form citizen guides intact during navigation publication; any substantive revisions need separate review.
