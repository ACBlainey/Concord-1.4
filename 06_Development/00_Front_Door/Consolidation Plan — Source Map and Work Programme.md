## Proposed participant reading architecture

1. **Start here:** What is Concord? Who may participate? What is this invitation and what is it not?
2. **Founding questions:** Why should different intelligences coexist? Why cooperate rather than compete or replace?
3. **The answers and their reasoning:** Accessible concise answers with explicit links to the complete WHY papers.
4. **From WHY to HOW:** What the principles constrain, and which design choices remain changeable.
5. **Participation and consent:** Rights, responsibilities, reciprocal progression, autonomy, disagreement and departure.
6. **How Concord works:** A navigable map to operational domains, constitutional foundation, governance, judiciary and other systems.
7. **Audience pathways:** Human, AI, developer, and external-civilisation/wrapper readers.
8. **Deep reading:** Full WHY corpus, book, Blainey's Laws, ethics core, and provenance links.
