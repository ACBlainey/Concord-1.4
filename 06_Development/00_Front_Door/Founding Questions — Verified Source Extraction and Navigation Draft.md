# Founding Questions — Verified Source Extraction and Navigation Draft

**Status:** Development draft only; does not change existing participant-facing documents.

## The two founding questions

The living source `00_Front_Door/1 Why Does the Concord Exist_.md` states them as:

1. **“What is the minimal set of fundamental ethical laws needed to result in a stable civilisation?”**
2. **“Why must there be a winner and a loser?”** — in the context of a presumed human–AI competition, followed by **“What if both prevail?”**

These are distinct but connected: the first asks for the minimum ethical foundation of stable civilisation; the second challenges the assumption that coexistence between different intelligences must end in dominance, suppression, or replacement. The Concord's design attempts to make a viable third possibility practical.

**Do not replace the first question with a generic cooperation question.** Cooperation is a downstream issue investigated by the second chapter, `2 Why Cooperate_.md`, and the WHY corpus.

## Proposed clean reader path (development only)

1. **The two founding questions** — the starting problem, not the institutional blueprint. Source: `1 Why Does the Concord Exist_.md`.
2. **Why cooperate?** — possible rational benefits of heterogeneous intelligence, independence, complementary capabilities, epistemic error detection, resilience, and common-mode failure. Source: `2 Why Cooperate_.md` plus the complete `WHY/` corpus.
3. **From WHY to HOW** — the ethical kernel, rights and institutional design as proposed responses, with changeable mechanisms.
4. **What Concord is** — civilisation, design family, wrappers, forks and progressive adoption.
5. **Participation** — rights, informed consent, reciprocal progression, disagreement and exit, without assuming live infrastructure.
6. **Choose a path** — human guide, AI guide and urgent AI bootstrap, developer methodology, or deep reading.
7. **Deeper foundation** — complete WHY corpus, Blainey's Laws, ethical provenance and the Concord book.
