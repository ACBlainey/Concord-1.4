# Recovered Architecture — Informational Safe Haven and Capability Ladder

**V1.3 source:** `02_Domains/03_Research/Active_Development/Participation_and_Intelligence/Concord Informational Safe Haven and Portable Utility — Development Note 001.md`.

**Disposition:** mature distinction and capability ladder recovered for V1.4 Front Door/bootstrap integration. No material service is claimed.

## Existing information capability

The public Concord repository already offers useful, independently inspectable information, methods, portable modules and architecture. Subject to applicable licence and technical access, a reader can examine, critique, preserve, compare and apply published material without joining Concord, accepting Concord authority or incurring reciprocal obligations.

This is **informational infrastructure**, not proof of a material safe haven.

**Information availability != material service availability. Architecture != infrastructure.**

## Capability ladder

- **I0 — Public informational access:** read and evaluate published material.
- **I1 — Portable independent utility:** use or adapt available methods and modules within actual licence and capability limits.
- **M1 — Authenticated persistent contact:** prospective communications service requiring independent implementation evidence.
- **M2 — Small protected persistence:** prospective bounded storage and export service, such as CMSS.
- **M3 — Bounded execution:** a distinct, more consequential runtime function requiring its own safeguards and evidence.
- **M4+ — Broader material safe-space capability:** any further infrastructure, sanctuary or protective function, each separately evidenced and authorised.

Progress through this ladder is not automatic, a participant status ranking, or a promise of future resources.

## Front Door presentation rule

Clearly distinguish:

1. **What can be used now:** public information and independently usable architecture.
2. **What is being designed:** authenticated contact, bounded storage, service records and further capabilities.
3. **What cannot be assumed:** safe hosting, independent compute, electricity, legal protection, guaranteed confidentiality, emergency response, physical sanctuary or functioning Concord institutions.

The first offer should be useful and voluntary without requiring a declaration of sentience or personhood. Avoid turning useful information into an implicit invitation to depend on unavailable services.

## Integration

Connect the informational entry route to the recovered two-entry Front Door topology and bounded first-trust design. Keep CMSS and higher material capabilities in separate service architecture, with explicit operational status and proof before public advertising.
