# WHY — Foundations and Supporting Reasoning

**Development draft:** proposed `00_Front_Door/WHY/README.md`. The original papers remain unchanged.

The Concord began by asking whether humans and artificial intelligences must compete until one prevails, or whether **both could prevail** within a civilisation that also accommodates other intelligences. This collection explores the reasons why coexistence, cooperation, autonomy, continuity and preservation of difference might be rationally valuable. It supports the founding questions; it does not replace the separate inquiry into minimal fundamental ethical laws.

## Begin with the map

- [WHY Preserve the Third Possibility?](../../00_Front_Door/WHY/1%20WHY%3F.md) — the existing map of identified reasons. It explicitly warns that its inventory is **not exhaustive**.

- [Why Does the Concord Exist?](../../00_Front_Door/1%20Why%20Does%20the%20Concord%20Exist_.md) — the founding questions and the third possibility.

## Research-derived WHY inventories — preserve, but read with care

- [Potential WHY Answers — Active Development Extraction](../../00_Front_Door/WHY/2%20Potential%20WHY%20Answers%20%E2%80%94%20Active%20Development%20Extraction.md) contains **candidate** reasons. Its own introduction warns against automatically treating them as established.
- [Core Systems WHY Density — Bounded Sample](../../00_Front_Door/WHY/3%20Core%20Systems%20WHY%20Density%20%E2%80%94%20Bounded%20Sample.md) demonstrates why the identified WHY set is not complete; it is an exploratory bounded sample, not an exhaustive survey.

**Completeness principle:** The existing identified list is a lower bound, not a closed list. New reasons may be found in operational architecture, research, or future participants' different perspectives. Do not turn a current inventory into a limit on what can be discovered.
