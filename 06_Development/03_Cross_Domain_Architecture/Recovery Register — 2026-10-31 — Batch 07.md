## Cross-domain boundaries retained for reconciliation

1. **Map and measurement are not authority.** Presence, maturity, sufficiency, consequence and validation remain distinct. A high score, model convergence or epistemic observation does not create a vote, mandate or intervention power.
2. **Clock trigger versus action authority.** Triggers can schedule review; owning domains retain substantive authority. Consequence ordering is not automatically civil causality. These sources do not change the independent emergency state machine or make emergency action wait for Clock progress.
3. **Knowledge dependency versus power.** KCS records relations and routes selective review/correction. A reference or dependency does not grant permission, authority or a right to another domain's data. Preserve bounded visibility, change materiality, unknown/disputed state and correction provenance.
4. **Wrappers and summits.** Functional interoperability, permission, standing, responsibility and recognition must not collapse into a general jurisdiction. Negotiation or attendance does not create authority to commit the Concord independently of the applicable decision architecture.
5. **Divergence and continuity.** The invention-frontier sources preserve separate local event validity, shared consequence finality, meaning/presentation, handover and reachability. Frozen independently generated candidate status is retained; no claim of novelty or implementation completeness is added.
6. **Stewardship progression.** Participation, standing, qualification, eligibility, selection, appointment, mandate and authority remain separate. The source contains developed ascension/evaluation and shadow-role interfaces but leaves constitutional/operational decisions open, including who legitimately selects/appoints. Historical references to earlier Governance issue registers are preserved as source text, not new instructions to reopen those audits or amend Governance V2.2.
7. **Source status language.** Historical 'canonical', 'validated', 'tested' or 'current' wording remains unchanged. This pass verifies copy integrity only; it does not independently validate those claims or promote source designs into operational authority.
