## Boundaries requiring particular care

### Epistemic observation and governance authority

Observation, divergence detection, independent evidence, confidence, model agreement and escalation for attention must not be treated as a governance decision, authority activation or an extra vote. This applies when comparing these sources with the previously recovered triadic epistemic/GTP papers.

The recovered competence source explicitly routes from function, competence evidence, dependencies, evaluation limits and consequence to the relevant authority architecture; information access, credentials, fluent output or apparent competence do not themselves grant authority. This recovery does not alter Governance V2.2 voting, appointments, emergency states or bounded functional authority.

### Cross-domain dependency attribution

The prospective dependency paper separates a missing owner/function, an integration failure and insufficient maturity of an identified owner. It proposes testing upstream development before creating duplicate downstream subsystems. Its Civilisation Clock example remains a candidate ownership claim requiring verification; it does not give the Clock emergency authority or make emergency state transitions dependent on clock progress.

### Founding and external interfaces

The Nation Foundation workstream separates design/publication from consent, ratification and legitimate jurisdiction. Its founder-power research concerns political/institutional authority; it does not amend Citizen Zero's root-level authorial editing rule or place that rule inside operational architecture.

The disputed-status wrapper design separates participant interpretation, operational interface, reserved claims and non-inference conditions. Interoperability does not silently settle sovereignty or civil status. The asylum paper separately examines claimant protection, method of arrival and a helper's intervention authority; it explicitly leaves the constitutional conflict unresolved.

### Foundational reasoning and empirical claims

Generative Closure and Epistemic Network are preserved as developed foundational reasoning and hypotheses, with their caveats. Their archival experiment labels are not grounds to lose them, but copying them into Development does not conclude the original experiment or authorise operational Front Door publication.
