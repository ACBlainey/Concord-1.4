# Architecture Recovery — Connector, Sketch Designs and Cross-Domain Source Corpus

**Date:** 31 October 2026. **Batch:** 06. **Rule:** Preserve first; reconcile second.

49 intact original files (910,857 bytes) have been preserved in Development. Every destination Git blob SHA-1 was fetched after creation and matched the supplied V1.3 snapshot bytes. V1.3 and operational V1.4 documents were not edited. Recovery is not adoption, evidence of correctness, or a completed duplication/coverage review.

## Scope and maturity

- **42 cross-domain root documents:** complete architecture, source-resolution, candidate specification and integration sources from `03_Cross_Domain_Architecture/`, preserved under the Development `Recovered_V1.3_Source_Corpus/` folder. This is a preservation selection, not a full substantive audit of every source. Nested source folders and test/audit records remain for further review; no completeness claim is made for the cross-domain corpus.
- **One connector source:** the complete xAI/Grok v0.1 development note, including provenance design, multi-provider controller/adapters, raw preservation, blinding/evaluation/unsealing and declared limitations. Historical pilot history is retained because it is embedded in the implementation source. This recovers a document, not the executable connector or its local logs. No API calls or experiments were run.
- **Six sketch sources:** recursive Civil Attention, privacy-preserving civic sensors, weighted epistemic memory, AI Gym, AI worker scarcity/replication and inverse-ethics research planning. These contain architectural possibilities, process boundaries or developed research programmes worth preserving. They remain sketches/hypotheses; no maturity elevation is implied. The two short sketches on ethical complexity/social scale and participant-serving civilisation remain in V1.3 pending later review, not rejected.

## Important cross-domain boundaries

| Recovered family | Integration/reconciliation boundary |
|---|---|
| Clinical competence and knowledge-learning interfaces | Health specifies clinical functions; Education owns learning/assessment; Historical preserves knowledge/provenance. Qualification or expertise does not itself confer clinical/legal authority. |
| Exceptional mental-health handoff | Preserve distinctions between clinical evidence/treatment, legal authority, judicial review and Civil Security functions. An evidence supplier is not automatically the authority holder. No new clinical procedure is activated. |
| Established life support, scarcity and withdrawal | Retain competing authority models and their caveats; aggregate benefit does not itself settle withdrawal authority. The source explicitly leaves a specialist Health rule missing. Recovery does not fill that gap. |
| Multi-key authority, route/state/context, execution conformance | Preserve independent required authority checks, scope, current state and action-specific consequence. Technical capability, evidence, route discovery or model agreement is not authority. |
| Authored risk, contextual participation and consent over time | Preserve participant authorship, protective duties, protected context, current consent/withdrawal and review boundaries without silently adopting any candidate rule. |
| Derived/correlated/shared-subject information and protection lineage | Intended interfaces include privacy, Research, Historical, KCS and authority. Transformation/recombination does not automatically remove protection or authorise access. |
| Dependencies, state propagation and infrastructure continuity | Reconcile legitimate ownership, non-transitive dependencies and bounded invalidation/transition rather than propagating authority across relationships. |
| Connector and methodological infrastructure | First-response, retry/error and model/protocol provenance remain distinct. Provider-reported model identity is not independent proof of backend execution. Schema-compliant output is not ethical assurance. |
| Sketch extensions | Internal reporting must not silently rewrite Civil Attention core; a sensor proposal does not grant surveillance authority; AI Gym/memory/replication proposals remain untested and require source resolution. |

Observation, prediction, epistemic weight, confidence, assessment and escalation for attention must remain distinct from governance decisions, votes and authority activation. No changes to Governance V2.2 or emergency-state architecture were made.

## Deferred/excluded scope

Raw participant evidence and standalone test/scenario packages remain in V1.3. Test/audit titles were used only to bound this batch; they are not grounds to exclude a file permanently. Further targeted review must recover any unique developed amendment embedded in those records.

The nine `Unified_Paths_Research_Offshoots` files were identified as short hypotheses/prospective research registers and remain in V1.3 in this pass. Their developed independent architectural relevance has not been established; they have not been declared disposable. The explicit exclusion of the Unified Paths of Light corpus remains in force and does not automatically exclude every independent offshoot.
