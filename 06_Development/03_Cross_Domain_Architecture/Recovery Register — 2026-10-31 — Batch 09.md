## Reconciliation boundaries

- Review composition must preserve separate grounds and substantive owners; shared evidence/work does not merge authority.
- Bounded discovery and termination are not proof of evaluation-space completeness, permission for surveillance or a universal veto through unknown unknowns.
- Derived information, participant-supporting infrastructure, scarcity, withdrawal and temporal consent retain their original refinements and unresolved domain interfaces. This pass adopts no clinical, liability, allocation or compelled-support rules.
- Observation, assessment, prediction, historical test success and confidence do not create governance votes, emergency authority or new jurisdiction.
- Clock scheduling and consequence ordering do not replace an independently legitimate emergency state machine.
- Existing source-corpus copies and extracts may duplicate adopted functionality; reconciliation is intentionally deferred rather than risking loss.
