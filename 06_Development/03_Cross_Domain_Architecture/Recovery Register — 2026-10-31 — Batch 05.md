## Cross-domain boundaries and integration tasks

- **Observation/model/prediction is not authority.** Preserve distinct information and authority flows when reconciling predictive support, counterfactual modelling, steering and exceptional access with Governance V2.2. Prediction is not guilt or a vote; a simulated act is not an act committed. Emergency authority remains independently grounded and must not be made dependent on Clock progress.
- **Support and investigation:** preserve the support-to-investigation firewall, purpose-limited information, controlled identity bridges, correction, access provenance and independent oversight. Participant refusal is not automatically incapacity or suspicion.
- **Metrics and learning:** connect Research, Historical, support domains, resource coordination and knowledge architecture without turning aggregate metrics or digital twins into general participant classifications or automatic interventions.
- **Investigative modelling:** the complete source is preserved, including its explicit HIGH-RISK RESEARCH / UNTESTED / NOT READY FOR OPERATIONAL ADOPTION classification and its independent-evidence/authority requirements. Recovery does not declare this mechanism legitimate or ready.
- **Machine ethics:** comprehension, interpretation, motivation, compatibility, assurance, accountability, standing, capacity and responsibility remain separate. Ethical mediation must not become unrestricted guardian sovereignty. Perpetual creator liability is a preserved hypothesis requiring examination, not an adopted rule or ownership/control right.
- **Ethical Guardian methods:** reference expectations are not ethical authority; participant judgement is distinct from articulation and schema compliance. Decision-object alignment, representation provenance, substrate translation, attention and participant state matter. Expertise is evidence, not ethical infallibility or constitutional authority.
- **Protocol maturity:** usability design and an experimental framework are substantive reusable design, not proof that the instrument or ethical theory is validated. No participant experiments were run, no external services contacted and no test result was elevated into operational authority.
