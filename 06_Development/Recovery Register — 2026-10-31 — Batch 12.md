Its safeguards distinguish ethical derivability from retroactive criminalisation and corrigibility from retroactive liability expansion. Preserve the source's then-current gap/priority claims for later comparison; they are not a new assessment that later Law files lack those mechanisms.
- **Infrastructure lineage:** the recovery source explicitly connects recoverable technology to skills, tooling, dependencies and spatial/environmental context rather than documents alone. It distinguishes nominal redundancy from independent supply and records maintenance and prevented events without claiming counterfactual certainty.
- **Defence/Civil Security:** development source registers preserve links to threat inquiry, accountable exceptional power, identity/evidence and shared steward-selection dependencies. Prediction, suspicion or evidential confidence does not create intervention authority.
- **Planning:** preserve substrate-aware environmental compatibility, accessibility, proportionate intervention, interface ownership and affected-participant review.
- **Domain handoffs:** Law, Judiciary, Governance, Health, Infrastructure, Historical, Continuity, Education and Civil Security retain distinct substantive functions. Copying these sources into Development does not alter their mandates or merge epistemic observation with governance decisions.
