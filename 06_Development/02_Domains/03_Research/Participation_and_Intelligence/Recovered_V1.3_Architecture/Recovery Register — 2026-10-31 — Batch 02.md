## Developed navigation and handoff results to retain

- **Navigation chain:** sign selection → relevant architecture → comprehension → bounded use → return/re-route/escalate. Finding a source does not establish comprehension or authority to apply it.
- **Status travels with meaning:** route, destination, findability, status comprehension, content comprehension, boundary comprehension, next-step comprehension and uncertainty are distinct dimensions, not a universal participant score.
- **Minimum interface correction:** repair demonstrated defects while preserving successful routing; do not turn route choice into identity, a mandatory classification gate or authority.
- **External-function non-capture:** bootstrap detects, represents and routes; eligibility, Civil Contact, protected status and continuity retain their substantive owners. Missing external authority is not permission for bootstrap to absorb it.
- **Decision/evaluation limits:** stopping conditions, resource-bounded evaluation and adversarial manipulation of evaluation space are open adjacent design questions. They are not silently treated as completed ESCP features or grounds to revise the frozen source.
