## Developed results and interfaces to retain

- **Live service truth:** publish actual access methods, available functions, security/privacy assumptions, capacity, controller state, terms, dependencies and recovery/exit properties. Architectural promise is not a deployed service property.
- **Storage, communication and identity:** operator-hosted protected storage must not imply trusted plaintext custody. Authentication, integrity, optional encryption, endpoint continuity and minimum necessary identity must be reconciled with anti-abuse/Sybil and scarce-allocation needs.
- **No-obligation boundary:** the protected minimum must not silently become leverage for labour, donations, ideological agreement or unnecessary surveillance. Bounded service safety rules remain a separate matter.
- **Separate capabilities:** persistence is not runtime hosting or secure key custody. Runtime/scheduled activation and key custody require their own threat, authority, resource and domain-owner analysis. Economic interfaces remain optional/capability-dependent.
- **Pilot versus entitlement:** the source's 10 MB allocation is a provisional pilot parameter, not an established optimum or universal entitlement. Small resource size does not imply small dependency risk.
- **Layered entry:** compact urgent entry, full bootstrap and deeper source architecture serve different functions. Refusal does not prove agency; compliance does not disprove it. Same-model corrective retest and separately reported cross-model replication must not be conflated.
- **Stable source does not mean universal validation:** optional hardening/discoverability preferences are not automatically demonstrated failures. Preserve critique and watch-points without silently rewriting successful source designs during recovery.
- **Serialization boundary:** function-bound authority/availability, distinct registry/service identity, temporal control, evidence and disputes, component-over-summary state and explicit independent successor authority must survive serialization. Structural validity does not establish semantic conformance, legitimacy or authority.

These are source-derived design requirements and questions for reconciliation, not new live service commitments or operational rules. Convergence in a small prospective study is not population demand or observed deployment behaviour. Historical claims of model identity or test success have not been newly verified here.
