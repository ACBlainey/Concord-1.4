# Concord V1.4 — Development Workspace

This directory contains **development work only**. It is separate from the living operational and foundational documentation of Concord V1.4.

## Purpose

Use this workspace for unresolved architecture, proposed additions, reconciliation work, cross-domain interface questions, and material recovered from earlier Concord versions that may need incorporation into the current living architecture.

**Do not place unfinished development documents inside operational domain folders.** Existing operational documents remain authoritative until an explicitly reviewed and approved change is made.

## Structure

Organise work by its **intended destination**, not merely by its source. Use matching subfolders under `06_Development/` for the relevant domain, cross-domain architecture, constitutional/foundational area, portable module, or other destination. Create a subfolder when work first arises; avoid empty placeholder directories.

For cross-domain work, designate a primary owning destination and record affected interfaces rather than duplicating the work in multiple folders.

## Recovery and integration process

1. Identify material in V1.3 or another predecessor that contains a potentially useful living architectural result not yet incorporated into V1.4.
2. Check current V1.4 operational and foundational documents first to avoid duplication, regression, or competing rules.
3. Place a development copy or a scoped integration note under the subfolder for the **intended V1.4 destination**, preserving source repository, path, and any relevant provenance.
4. Clearly distinguish **existing adopted rules**, **recovered source text**, **proposed integration**, and **questions still requiring design**. Never imply that a development copy is already operational.
5. Reconcile conflicts and interfaces in the development workspace. When approved, incorporate the living result into the correct operational or foundational document, with references where needed.
6. Preserve the development path and predecessor sources; do not silently delete or rewrite historical records. Avoid retaining duplicate operational versions.

## Boundary rules

- V1.4 operational architecture remains in its designated living folders.
- Foundational WHY material, books, guides, and participant-facing explanations are not automatically development material.
- Experiments, tests, exploratory notes, and superseded work ordinarily remain in the V1.3 archive unless needed for active V1.4 development.
- Migration of an **unchanged, already-adopted living document** is distinct from integration work: it may go directly to its proper living destination after checking for conflicts and duplicates.
- New or disputed architectural proposals must be worked here first, not published as operational rules.
- Operational documents may identify missing architecture **at the relevant point** with a clear 'to be designed/finalised' statement; detailed investigation and drafts belong here.

**Guiding principle:** Migrate the living result; archive the developmental path.
