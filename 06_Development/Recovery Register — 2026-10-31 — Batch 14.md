## Developed cross-domain results retained

- **Protected information transfer:** classify transfer itself as a bounded interoperability state; source labels do not create extraterritorial sovereignty, but removing labels does not erase agreed obligations. Projection/query-only alternatives, disputed protection and minimum disclosure remain available design options.
- **Mental/cognitive Health:** preserve legacy/source-resolution and exceptional-authority distinctions. Clinical evidence and treatment expertise are not a legal sovereignty, automatic incapacity verdict or blanket consent. The Health audit's gap priorities are historical development claims requiring comparison with later work, not newly asserted current omissions.
- **Education:** access, representational coverage, understanding, application, competence, tool-assisted/composite capability and resilience remain distinct. Evidence of competence does not itself confer office or authority. Empirical validation of assessment claims remains separate from their design.
- **Environment:** preserve temporal/spatial references, instrument/method versions, calibration, uncertainty, coverage, baseline and interpretation when comparing environmental states. Historical, Research, Metrics and Environment retain distinct custody/method/measurement/domain-meaning functions. Measurement differences do not automatically establish causal responsibility.
- **Participant support:** autonomy-preserving support and capacity/authority boundaries must survive later integration with Health, Social Support, Civil Attention and continuity. A need or prediction does not create unrestricted intervention authority.
- **Culture:** Historical preserves cultural evidence; Culture concerns living voluntary cultural activity. Preservation does not require freezing a practice, and cultural consequences do not authorise compulsory cultural engineering.

Original source labels, open questions and historical references remain unchanged. Recovery of an earlier V1.2-derived synthesis does not import the entire V1.2 development archive or settle independent research claims.
