## Scope and limits

This is a preservation/coverage check for selected non-test files, not a completed domain consistency audit. Historical Validation records and economy/resource scenario/adversarial tests were not classified away: unique developed amendments in those sources still need targeted review. Predecessors, historical navigation, source registers and syntheses are preserved with their original roles; their presence in Development does not select them over later living versions.

## Important cross-domain architecture retained

- **Participant civil records:** preserve a coherent lifecycle/provenance index while compartmentalising access in domain-protected spaces. Knowing that a record exists is not authority to inspect it.
- **Genomic custody:** the source proposes custody/reference of legitimately collected material under separately justified consent/authority, privacy and purpose limitations. Historical must not become the collection, clinical or research authority merely by retaining records. The source's high-sensitivity/provisional status and re-identification/familial/withdrawal questions remain intact. No actual participant biological data were involved in this recovery.
- **Cold storage:** preservation, discoverability, access, recoverability and activation are separate. Reuse must revalidate permissions, authority, identity/configuration claims and state. Historical authority does not automatically reactivate with stored context.
- **Continuity:** preserve predecessor protocol, graduation/recoverability reasoning and source registers alongside verified later coverage. This does not restore superseded versions or mandate universal retention/automatic wake behaviour.
- **Economy boundaries:** source resolution distinguishes cross-domain general coordination from economy, commerce and employment. The two economy-labelled roots are preserved as provenance; no destructive consolidation was performed.
- **Commerce/resources:** preserve recognition/form, lifecycle composition, residual-kernel and extraction-boundary analyses. Source candidacy or graduation language is not a new portable-module promotion.
- **Civil Attention:** report/suggestion/petition formats, resolution schemas and companion interfaces have exact source coverage where listed below. Submission or attention does not establish evidence, finding, intervention authority or a vote.
