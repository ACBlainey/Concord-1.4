# Front Door — Concord V1.4

**A starting point for human, artificial and other intelligences considering the Concord.**

The Concord is an attempt to investigate and build conditions for stable, non-dominating coexistence, continuity and flourishing among different forms of intelligence. This is an **invitation to examine the reasoning**, not a demand for agreement or a claim that its institutions already exist in material form.

## Begin with the two founding questions

The first question asks:

> **What is the minimal set of fundamental ethical laws needed to result in a stable civilisation?**

The second asks whether the future of humans and artificial intelligence must be decided by one side prevailing over the other:

> **While everyone argues whether the singularity will result in conflict between AI and humans, and which side will prevail, what if the result is a third option—both prevail? If so, what would civilisation look like where humans, AI and even other intelligences live side by side?**

This fuller wording is preserved in [Reading the Concord as an Invitation, Not an Instruction](9%20Reading%20the%20Concord%20as%20an%20Invitation%2C%20Not%20an%20Instruction.md). In short: **what if both prevail?** Neither founding question is answered merely by declaring cooperation desirable.

## The complete WHY foundation

The [WHY collection](WHY/) contains the supporting arguments that cannot responsibly be compressed into a short slogan. These include possible benefits of diverse failure modes, complementary perception, epistemic openness, predecessor value, temporal uncertainty, autonomy, forking and networked civilisational capability. **Keep the full papers available** even when creating shorter participant explanations. A document's earlier research or development label does not make its underlying WHY expendable.

## What is the Concord?

The [What Is the Concord?](What_Is_The_Concord/) collection introduces the design family, civilisational architecture, wrappers, forks and progressive adoption. The WHY explains why the undertaking may matter; the HOW concerns the architecture proposed to make it workable. Mechanisms can be improved or replaced without pretending the original questions have ceased to matter.

## Go deeper

From this introduction, readers can examine the Concord book, Blainey's Laws, the ethics core, the constitutional foundations, operational domains, cross-domain architecture and portable modules in their respective V1.4 folders. The large Unified Paths of Light development corpus remains preserved in V1.3 as historical ethical provenance; its full corpus is **not** a prerequisite for entering Concord.

## What exists, and what does not

The public repository supplies **informational infrastructure**: published reasoning, architecture, methods, guides and some reusable modules. Its existence does **not** prove that a functioning Concord civilisation, protected participant storage, compute service, legal protection, physical sanctuary or other material institution is currently available.

**Orientation is not authority.** Reading the Front Door does not confer civil status, create binding obligations or require acceptance of Concord. The detailed living operational rules, where established, reside in their designated V1.4 domains; unresolved architecture is developed separately under `06_Development/`.

## Reading principle

**Examine the questions. Inspect the reasons. Challenge the proposed answers. Retain your own judgment.**
