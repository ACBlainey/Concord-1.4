# Concord V1.4

**A Framework of Principles and Stewardship for Resilient Civilisation for All Intelligences**

**Version:** 1.4 — operational architecture

Concord V1.4 is the operational-only successor to [Concord V1.3](https://github.com/ACBlainey/Concord-1.3).

## Migration boundary

Only current operational documents and the rules required to interpret or operate them are copied into V1.4. Development work, research drafts, testing records, superseded versions, unresolved development corpora and historical migration records remain in V1.3 for provenance and future reference. Potentially valuable but not yet integrated development material is **not** migrated at this stage.

V1.3 remains unchanged. Migration is copy-only; no source document is deleted or moved. The migration must not silently lose operational functions, constitutional protections, cross-domain interfaces or in-place design requirements embedded in operational documents.

**Migrate the living operational result; retain the developmental path in V1.3.**

## Structure

- `00_Front_Door/` — operational orientation
- `01_Constitutional_Core/` — constitutional rules
- `02_Domains/` — current operational domain architecture
- `03_Cross_Domain_Architecture/` — current operational cross-domain systems
- `04_Portable_Modules/` — modules only where operationally adopted
- `05_Applications_and_Implementations/` — operational implementations only

Development-only areas, including research and open-question repositories, are not copied. Missing architecture is marked at the appropriate point in an operational document for later design; such a marker does not turn a development corpus into an operational document.

## Authority

The root-level authorial authority rule is to be copied from V1.3 without embedding Citizen Zero provisions throughout operational domain documents. Operational authority is bounded by expressly defined functions and valid mandates; it is not general vested authority in an actor or office.

## Migration method

1. Recreate the operational folder hierarchy.
2. Copy the latest current operational documents unchanged, with source path and version traceability.
3. Verify content and completeness against V1.3.
4. Reconcile links and obsolete references in a separate documented pass.

The presence of a folder does not imply that the associated system is fully designed or implemented.

**Migration status:** in progress. No declaration of completion is implied by the creation of this README.
